ABOUT

Made in South Africa, for South African businesses

Hi, I'm Edward. I build websites and web apps, and Lost Lantern Digital is where I make tools for the small businesses around me.

Again and again I saw barbers, tutors and coaches running their whole day from WhatsApp chats and paper diaries: bookings lost in a scroll, two clients for one slot, and no clear total at the end of the month. So I started building simple, affordable apps that fix exactly that, made for the way South African businesses really work.

Some ideas get lost. We bring them to light.
