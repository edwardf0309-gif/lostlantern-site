QUESTIONS

Questions people ask

Will it work for my kind of business?

Morrow is made for businesses that work by appointment, with one person or a small team: barbers, hair and beauty salons, tutors, personal trainers and coaches. If your day is a list of clients booked with a person at a time, Morrow fits.

What does it cost?

Morrow is free while it's in early access, and you don't need a card to sign up. Before any pricing starts, we'll tell you at least 30 days in advance, and you decide whether to stay. Nothing is ever charged without your agreement.

Is my clients' information safe?

Yes. Everything is stored securely online and every connection is encrypted. Only people you invite can see your business, and staff logins never see pay. You can download a backup or delete your data whenever you like. Read more in our privacy policy.

Will someone help me set it up?

Yes. During early access we'll help you get going: adding your services, staff and existing clients. Just send us a message.
